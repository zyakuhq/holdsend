# Code of Conduct

HoldSend adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, for every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, discussions and any chat or event.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback about the work rather than the person, and do not harass anyone. The full text at the link above is the one that applies.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **security@zyaku.com** with "Code of conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the project maintainers and kept confidential. Maintainers who are part of a report will not handle it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban), choosing the response that fits what happened.
