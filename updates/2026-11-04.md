# Update, Wed 4 Nov 2026

## Done today
- Phase 3 started in code: `readDraft` in `@holdsend/core/drafts` turns a draft's update log into its open heads. Two edits on one base stay as two heads until a person resolves them. Nothing is merged and no winner is picked ([#46](https://github.com/zyakuhq/holdsend/pull/46), [decision 0009](https://github.com/zyakuhq/holdsend/blob/main/docs/decisions/0009-two-device-drafts.md)).
- Tests simulate three devices with lost answers and retries, and check the result against a record kept apart from the code. Four broken readers (last writer wins, retry counted twice, stale edit dropped, loose resolve) each fail them.
- A separate reviewer found seven problems, including a reader and a history helper that disagreed on a repeated id. All fixed.

Today HoldSend gained that reader: when two devices edit one draft offline, both edits stay as open heads until a person chooses. Nothing merges by itself and nothing is lost.
