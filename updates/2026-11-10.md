## LinkedIn post

Today I measured something I had only assumed: what a long offline queue costs on a phone. HoldSend's React Native store keeps the queue as one JSON string. That makes saves atomic and simple, but every change rewrites all of it. In a laptop test, 1,000 small notes wrote about 900 MB in total, while the number of writes stayed at three per item.

The lesson: a design that is safe and simple still has a cost curve, and it is better to publish the numbers than to let someone find them on a cheap phone. I wrote the results and the advice (keep queues short, keep files out of payloads) into the docs, and added a test so the cost cannot quietly get worse.
