Emergency messages

A demo of HoldSend's emergency queue. It is not an emergency service and contacts no one. In an emergency, call your local emergency number first.

Write a short message with or without a connection. The first message is sent by itself when the connection returns. Later ones wait until you press Send, so a stale message is not sent without your press.
